## Internal red-team review

- **Confirmation bias:** Explicitly searched against 4–6 and sparse dialogue. [S04](https://doi.org/10.1177/009365088015003001) is a material counterfinding; a 5–7 core remains a real alternative. 3–4 may fit if content becomes simpler and more ritual-based. No head-to-head age preference was found.
- **Concept protection:** Pip/Nimbus retains serious power, physics, originality and formula risks. Fix-It could win on controlled action; Lumina could win on calm parent positioning. Neither alternative was fully developed or dismissed.
- **Market claim quality:** No underserved niche or global market size was asserted. UK survey bins and self-reported rights-holder scale are labeled. Competitor sample is deliberately non-exhaustive.
- **Child science:** Differentiated CDC milestones, experiments, reviews and creative inferences. No invented viewing-attention seconds. Studies of repeated viewing are not mislabeled as voluntary demand evidence. Pacing review is mixed.
- **Canon and originality:** All new creative content lives in a separate proposal; existing show/story statuses are untouched. No plots or distinctive gags were reproduced. Eight premise tests remain examples only.
- **Source limitations:** Several studies were assessed through abstracts. Source dates can drift. No quantitative original dataset, child test, originality clearance or tool benchmark was performed. These gaps prevent Canon v1.0 and production lock.

**Decision readiness:** Sufficient to present a bounded Show Bible v0.1 proposal for owner review; insufficient to approve canon or claim commercial/product feasibility. Next step is owner review plus a research plan for child/caregiver, originality and production tests.
