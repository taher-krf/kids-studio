# Name and Neighbor Screen 002 (desk-level)

**Status: DEVELOPMENT RESEARCH, NOT LEGAL CLEARANCE.** This is a targeted desk screen supporting the [Show Bible v0.2 draft](../01_show_bible/SHOW_BIBLE_V0.2_DEVELOPMENT.md) under `DEC-0018`, recorded as `RES-0008`. Date: 2026-09-26 (Europe/Istanbul). Method: a small number of targeted web searches per candidate name, plus concept-neighbor searches. No trademark database search, no design-rights review, no counsel. It builds on [Name Screen 001](STRESS_TEST_001/ORIGINALITY_NAME_SCREEN.md).

## 1. Naming criteria

- Short: two syllables or fewer.
- Easy for 3–6-year-olds to say.
- Consonants and vowels common across major languages. Avoid "r," "th" and clusters where possible.
- Not a generic descriptive word ("Cloudy," "Nimbus," "Puff").
- No unfortunate meanings in major languages.
- Searchable and distinctive as a pair.
- The pair should *sound* like its shape contrast: soft and round for the cloud, crisp for the armored friend (bouba/kiki).

## 4. Concept-neighbor findings (hook check)

A search for feelings-as-weather cloud characters found:

- the generic **Personal Raincloud** trope ([TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Main/PersonalRaincloud));
- **C.J.** in *Regular Show*, a cloud character whose anger becomes storms ([Fandom](https://hero.fandom.com/wiki/Cloudy_Jay)). She is adult-skewing, and her weather is severe.
- a *Teen Titans Go!* cloud character ([Fandom](https://teen-titans-go.fandom.com/wiki/Cloud));
- Nickelodeon's **Middlemost Post** rain-cloud lead ([Toy Insider](https://thetoyinsider.com/nickelodeon-middlemost-post-series/));
- **Cloud Boy** (picture book) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud_Boy));
- *The Marshmallow Times*' **Cloud**, an eager helper who wants to be useful ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Marshmallow_Times));
- Pixar's **Partly Cloudy** clouds ([Disney Wiki](https://disney.fandom.com/wiki/Clouds_(Partly_Cloudy))).

A search for an armadillo-and-cloud pairing found no existing children's property.

**Assessment:** "feelings show as weather" exists as a *trope* and as a temper gag in older-skewing comedy. The v0.2 combination is not occupied by any neighbor found here. That combination is a **preschool reading game** built on a mild, readable weather code, paired with a friend who **hides** feelings through behavior. Differentiation guardrails are recorded in [Show Bible v0.2 §23](../01_show_bible/SHOW_BIBLE_V0.2_DEVELOPMENT.md).
