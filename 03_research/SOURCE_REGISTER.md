## Use limits

The original studies' abstracts or institutional summaries were reviewed, not every full text. No audience experiment, channel analytics access, image generation benchmark, merch test, international survey, or legal clearance was performed. `S08` is an older study; contemporary application is an inference. `S18` and `S19` describe the UK. Source dates and policies should be refreshed before publishing or monetization decisions.
