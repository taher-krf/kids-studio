# YouTube and YouTube Kids: 2026 policy research

Status: RESEARCH SNAPSHOT, not legal advice or launch approval. Accessed **2026-09-25**. Official Google/YouTube sources are linked directly below; source metadata appears in [SOURCE_REGISTER](../SOURCE_REGISTER.md). Recheck every rule at upload time.

## Question, search, exclusions

What official rules constrain an original animated show directed at approximately ages 4–6? Searched YouTube Help/TeamYouTube pages on audience designation, Made for Kids features/ads, YouTube Kids selection, quality, monetization, disclosure and child safety. Accepted official [S20](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10938174?hl=en), [S21](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076?hl=en), [S22](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9632097?hl=en), [S23](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9713557?hl=en), [S24](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en), [S25](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10774223?hl=en-AU), [S26](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801999?hl=en-GB), [S27](https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/424874071?hl=en). Rejected third-party policy blogs and creator guesses about enforcement.

| Advertising | Personalized ads and remarketing are prohibited on Made for Kids; contextual ads can run subject to policy [S23](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9713557?hl=en). | AdSense-only economics are fragile; no revenue projection made; **HIGH / PRIMARY**. |

Voluntary transparency may serve trust, but the announcement is **not** an AI-animation ban; **HIGH / PRIMARY**. |

## AI-generated children's media: four distinct evidence types

**A. Official policy:** The rules above constrain originality, quality, safety and disclosure according to the content, not a blanket prohibition on AI children's animation [S24](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en), [S27](https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/424874071?hl=en). **B. Advocacy:** Fairplay and signatories called for much stricter AI-video rules in 2026; this is an advocacy request and concern, **not** current YouTube law or causal harm proof [S28](https://fairplayforkids.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/YouTube-Letter-AI-Slop.pdf). **C. Reporting:** AP documented the campaign and YouTube's response; it does not give a representative prevalence estimate [S29](https://apnews.com/article/68f866c48127222208e1d978b46cbc80). **D. Operational inference:** Inconsistent characters, physically impossible continuity, uncanny expressions, accidental unsafe imagery and generic mass output could erode trust and fail quality review. These are risks to benchmark, not measured current failure rates.

## Launch control hypothesis

For each finished candidate, a human reviews age designation, story/visual safety, metadata honesty, audio level, asset rights, originality, AI disclosure applicability and quality. Preserve prompt/reference/version and editorial change records. This is a proposed future control, not a publishing action taken in this sprint. Monetization or legal details should receive specialist review before launch.
