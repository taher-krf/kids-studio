## The question

Can the approved Pip + Nimbus development direction sustain a distinctive, rewatchable, non-formulaic series over a large catalog?

## The answer the evidence supports

**Conditionally yes — and the condition is specific and testable.** The concept as originally framed (organized planner + eager cloud + mild weather) fails: the unguarded baseline batch collapsed to 75% formula and 95% rejection, confirming the red team's diagnosis at generation level. The same concept **with the development hypotheses adopted** (Structure A contradictions, expressive power philosophy, character comedy engine E1–E4, third-force layer) produced 142 accepted, mechanism-distinct premises across all 33 story families, with 0% concentration in the three feared formulas, balanced agency, zero third-lead dependency, and the strongest material concentrated in the ownable expressive-power layer.

The durability is therefore **conditional, not intrinsic**: it lives in the hypotheses, not in the shell the owner originally approved. The decision this phase actually presents is whether to adopt those hypotheses.

## Outcome classification: **PROCEED WITH REPAIRS**

- Not PROCEED: the approved frame alone does not sustain the slate (G0 evidence); repairs are not optional.
- Not REWORK CONCEPT: the repairs are attachable specification layers, tested at premise level; the dyad, mild powers, and format survive.
- Not REOPEN ALTERNATIVES: controls show Fix-It is cheaper but thinner in relational engine; no creative evidence favors reopening (production economics remains the one live trigger — below).
- Not STOP: the falsification instrument returned a clear pass on its stated kill criterion (125 distinct signatures vs. the ~50 threshold).

## Repairs that the evidence supports (all require owner decision)

1. **Adopt the development hypotheses** (`DEVELOPMENT_HYPOTHESES.md`): hook candidate H1, contradiction Structure A, comedy engine E1–E4, expressive power philosophy (bounds unchanged), third-force layer T-A/T-B/T-C.
2. **Rename.** The screen found the exact name pair occupied in children's content; "Nimbus" is crowded and descriptive. Recommend renaming both leads before any visual design (new names screened).
3. **Rule the expressive-trigger scope.** Sleep-drizzle and involuntary physiology (sneeze-gusts) extend *triggers* without extending powers; owner ruling needed on whether this sits inside `DEC-0016`.
4. **Adopt two anti-formula rules** discovered by the test: (a) resolution diversity (no resolution type may dominate a slate — `understanding`+`reframe` hit 64% even here); (b) every episode carries one signature-engine beat (forecast irony or feelings-visible), not only generic trait comedy.
5. **Commission the weighted production benchmark** next: price the pool's actual burden mix (≈6 MEDIUM : 3 HIGH : 1 EXTREME per 10 episodes), weighted to vapor continuity, wet-state persistence, calibrated wind, prop contact, acting holds. This is the remaining gate where Fix-It could still win.
6. **Keep the T1–T10 child/caregiver battery** as the mandatory gate between any Show Bible v0.2 and Canon v1.0. Preference is still untested; this entire phase produced desk evidence only.

## What this phase did not prove

Episode quality, acting feasibility, child preference or comprehension, cross-cultural transfer, economics, legal clearance. The concept earned the right to the next gate; nothing more.
