## 7. Production burden — the correlation that matters

Accepted burden: LOW 4.2%, MEDIUM 58.5%, HIGH 30.3%, EXTREME 7.0%.

**Quality rises with burden**: mean review total LOW 9.50 → MEDIUM 9.93 → HIGH 9.93 → EXTREME **10.20**. Power-essential premises: 16 HIGH + 6 EXTREME of 30 (73%). The premise-level expression of red-team finding M2 is confirmed inside this pool: **the most distinctive, most ownable stories are systematically the hardest to produce** (vapor-continuity and wet-state flags concentrate in the expressive-power peaks: PN-1010, PN-3022, PN-3023, PN-5024 are EXTREME). This is not a reason to abandon them; it is the evidence that the production benchmark must be weighted to exactly these shots, and that a viable slate needs deliberate burden *mixing* (LOW/MEDIUM character episodes amortize the EXTREME peaks).
