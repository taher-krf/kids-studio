## 6. Review and findings (2026-09-26, reviewer: Kimi Cowork, frame-level)

### Character continuity
- **Mulu: holds.** Silhouette (flat base, tall Top Puff with front curl, two nubs), dark-oval eyes with highlights, chunky brows, cream matte material and the cool blue-grey underside band survive in every shot, including mid-motion frames. No limbs ever grown (D06 clean); no mirror flip of the Top Puff (D04 clean); no translucency (D07 clean). Issues: mild piled-puff proportion drift in SH03's final frames (D01), and his size relative to the chair shifts between SH03 (large) and SH04 (smaller) (D16). Known watch items persist (nubs elongated vs the tiny-stub spec).
- **Tekla: holds strongly.** Half-lidded deadpan face, head shield, tall ears, teal apron with pocket and yellow folding ruler all persist across SH02–SH05; the three-band logic and ringed tail read in SH04's side view (D11/D15 clean). She never deforms (D12 clean), never gets wet (n/a). Retained light sclera = known watch item, unchanged.
- **Scale relationship:** approximately right per shot (Mulu ≈ her height, wider), but drifts *between* shots (D16) — the tool recomputes scale per generation without an anchor.

### Prop continuity
- **Chair: holds.** The same simple light-wood pegged ladder-back design (matching the old chair in `R1.5_HILLTOP_3D_MASTER.jfif`) appears in all five shots, same material and proportions. **DEVELOPMENT PROP — NOT FINAL CANON** (`PRP-CHAIR-NEW` twin; W-2 prop sheet still pending).
- The planned *pairing* (two identical chairs side by side) was not achieved in either SH01 take — the tool places one chair per scene reliably but resists duplicating an identical prop at a directed position (class 2, prompt/control).

### World continuity
- Geography matches the Hilltop master in the wide (SH01 T02): tree stage-left, knoll + round door + umbrella bed centre, chimes post and Forecast Board left of the door (as in the master — the board-side owner ruling stays open), garden + pinwheel + bench stage-right, stream at the foot. No mirroring between shots (D18 clean in the selected takes); no text anywhere (D19 clean); no unplanned characters (D20 clean). SH02–SH05 share one consistent generated bench-zone environment. Day light held across the sequence.

### Motion
- Readable action in every shot; no AI morphing of identities; no sliding; Mulu's float/bob reads (V-01 grammar carries into scene work). Tekla's press in SH04 is physically clear. The *spring-back* (bounce to hover) does not happen — consistent with the smoke-test class-3 finding that sharp timing accents and state reversals are beyond the current tool. No unwanted camera drift; all cameras locked as directed.

### Cross-shot state continuity (the key evaluation target)
- **SH01 → SH02:** consistent (wide → medium, same world and day light; chair zone stage-right).
- **SH02 → SH03:** consistent (positions carry; Mulu moves from beside to above the chair — motivated).
- **SH03 → SH04:** consistent — accidentally. SH03's constraint decay (Mulu ends seated) happens to match SH04's generated start (seated), so the cut works.
- **SH04 → SH05:** **break.** SH04 ends with Mulu seated on the chair; SH05 starts with him hovering beside it. Root cause: shot start states are not controllable without start-frame conditioning; the tool only received design references, not the previous shot's end frame. **This is the specific production-control problem to solve before expanding generation** (candidate fix, zero-cost to test: feed the previous shot's final frame as a reference image for the next shot).

### Story and comedy (sound OFF)
- Tekla's intention (here is a chair, for you): clear in SH02 without words. Mulu's attempt: clear in SH03. The chair problem: visible via the mid-shot air gap. The joke **almost lands**: hover + press + shared stare all read; the *boing* payoff is missing, so the gag resolves soft instead of springing. Reaction holds are present and preschool-readable. Emotional read: Mulu's joy → pride → doubt and Tekla's deadpan throughout are legible at 48 px as well as full size.

### Overall verdict
**The connected sequence works at development level.** Five shots from one reference set hold both character identities, the chair, the world, the light and the screen direction — the first evidence that Kids Studio can produce a coherent sequence rather than isolated clips. Two problems are tool/process-side, not design-side: (a) multi-second constraint decay and missing timing accents (class 3), and (b) missing start-state chaining between shots (class 2/process — a concrete, testable fix exists). No redesign of Mulu, Tekla, the chair or the Hilltop is indicated by this evidence.
