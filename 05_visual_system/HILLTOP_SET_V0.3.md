# The Hilltop — Set Plan v0.3

**Status: DEVELOPMENT SET PLAN. NOT FINAL DESIGN, NOT CANON.** Proposed under `PROP-0008` within the owner-approved development direction (`DEC-0019`). Sketches: [master wide](sketches/hilltop_master_wide.svg) · [top-down set plan with cameras](sketches/hilltop_set_plan.svg). Characters: [visual development package](VISUAL_DEVELOPMENT_V0.3.md).

## 1. What the set is for

A small playground where hundreds of little stories can happen: **one hill, one stream, one garden, one tree and one door.** The Hilltop carries about 60% of a season's screen time from a handful of fixed camera setups. It should feel lived-in and handmade. It is not a world to explore; everything on it is a toy the story can pick up.

**Two fingerprints everywhere:** the land is soft and rolling (Mulu's shapes); everything built on it is crisp and pegged (Tekla's shapes). You can tell at a glance who made what.

## 2. Layout ([plan](sketches/hilltop_set_plan.svg))

Stage directions are used instead of a compass. **Downstage** is the valley view and village rooftops (the sunset side).

## 3. Element specs

Each element is designed once, with fixed states.

### Home base: the knoll and the round door
- A grassy rise with a **round wooden plank door** (a small round window optional) and a brass knob; pegged frame. The door is the biggest crisp shape on the hill.
- States: closed · open (warm interior light).
- Pair shot: Tekla at the door, Mulu at talk height beside it (HT-03).

### Umbrella bed
- An **upturned red umbrella on a wooden post**, like a bowl-shaped flower, with the hooked handle up. Mulu sleeps in the bowl.
- States: on post · carried by Mulu (bowl-up, beneath him; P3) · filling (¼ / ½ / ¾) · tipped and empty.
- It must read as an umbrella at every angle: ribs visible and the hooked handle.

### Forecast Board
- A small chalkboard on two pegged legs, with **two squares**: Mulu's (left) and Tekla's (right, always empty until the season finale).
- Chalk symbols (§6): sun, drizzle, gusts, single drop, plips, question mark.
- States: blank · one symbol · symbol + "?" · wet (P3-09, dries by the next scene).

### Wind chimes
- **Three spoons, two shells and one pebble** on strings from a driftwood stick, hung on Tekla's hook on a crooked post by the door.
- States: not yet made (before P3) · wrapped in a leaf · hung/still · ringing · spinning (feedback loop).
- Sound partner: each of Mulu's moods has its own chime pattern (Show Bible §18.2).

### Bench and the old chair
- A plain plank bench with a backrest, facing the valley. **Tekla's old chair** stands beside it: a pegged ladder-back chair, her first build.
- P1 adds its twin (the new chair) with four states.
- HT-02 is the emotional setup of the series: the sunset reverse (backs to camera) is used sparingly.

### Lookout Tree
- An old round-canopied tree at the stage-left edge, with **one long branch reaching over the hill: Mulu's branch** (his ceiling, ~4 TH).
- The fledgling's nest (P2) sits in the canopy.
- No climbing on screen (imitable danger). Tekla uses rope and a pulley (P1).

### Garden
- Rows of simple leafy tufts, a cabbage patch (P3), garden stakes, a **pinwheel on a stake** (background wind tell) and a pebble-bordered path.
- The **Snail** lives here: it has a small painted dot on its shell and must be findable in wides.

### Stream
- A clear blue ribbon at the foot of the hill with stepping stones, a little plank bridge and a bend where the Heron stands.
- Edge set for the three pilots; ready for "Something Came Down the Stream" and "Meant To Do That".

### Workshop (inside the knoll)
- One round room: a workbench, a pegboard of tool silhouettes, a bits bin, pictogram plans, a round window, and the **Keep Shelf**.
- The Keep Shelf is a single long shelf that gains **one souvenir per episode**. It is continuity-tracked in the shot manifest (`keep_shelf`).

## 5. Light and seasons

- **Standard lighting states:** morning (cool-warm, key from stage right), day (neutral, high sun), golden hour (warm, low sun setting downstage over the valley). Night is a showcase variant only.
- **Seasons are re-dress, not new sets:** blossom (spring), full green (summer), orange leaves on the tree and garden (autumn), bare branches with a pale hill (winter). Benchmark B9 re-dresses one setup to prove it.

## 6. Pictograms (a text-free world)

No letters or words anywhere. Every sign is a picture.

| Set | Symbols |
| --- | --- |
| Forecast Board | sun · drizzle lines · swirl gusts · single drop · two fat plips · question mark |
| Tekla's picture-tags | tools by silhouette · a closed eye ("not looking", P3) · a bird (the ramp, P2) · jars by contents |
| Village picture-notes | a muffin, a ladder, a clock face with hands (requests and deadlines) |
| Somebody Downstream | drawings on paper boats; never revealed |

## 7. What not to build

- No village set (rooftops only; grown-ups offscreen).
- No sky world above the Lookout branch (the Big Clouds are faceless weather).
- No new permanent locations before the core five (Hilltop, Garden, Workshop, Stream, Lookout Tree) are proven in production.
- No readable text, logos or brands in the world.

Machine-readable IDs for every element are in [ENVIRONMENT_MANIFEST.json](ENVIRONMENT_MANIFEST.json) and [PROP_MANIFEST.json](PROP_MANIFEST.json).
