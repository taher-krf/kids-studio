# Dialogue rules

Status: PROVISIONAL

Prefer visual clarity and limited, translatable dialogue. Dialogue may support emotion and comprehension; avoid exposition, lectures, and culturally narrow jokes.

Development draft (proposal `PROP-0007`, not canon): [Show Bible v0.2 §14](SHOW_BIBLE_V0.2_DEVELOPMENT.md#14-dialogue-approach).

**Approval:** Any promotion of a provisional detail to canon requires explicit owner decision and a new ledger entry.
