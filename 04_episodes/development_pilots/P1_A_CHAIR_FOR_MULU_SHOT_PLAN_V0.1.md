## How to read this plan

- **Sound-off first.** Every shot exists because something visually changes. Dialogue is sparse (the beat sheet's 20 words total) and is marked only where a line rides a shot; the plan must read with sound OFF.
- **Pacing.** Preschool holds: reactions get +6–12 frames over adult timing; jokes get a hold after landing; no social-media cutting. Average shot length ≈ 8.5 s over 42 shots / ~6:00.
- **Screen direction.** HT-01/HT-02 axis held: knoll + door upstage-centre, Lookout Tree stage-left, bench + chairs stage-right. Only the HT-02 reverse (SH40) deliberately reverses it, per the set plan.
- **Light track.** morning (S01) → day (S02–S08) → golden hour (S09–S16), per the beat sheet's state track.
- **Asset sufficiency.** "Assets" refers to the owner-confirmed local references (six R1.5 masters + thirteen R2 sheets, register: `05_visual_system/ASSET_REGISTRY.json`). SUFFICIENT = existing masters cover the shot's subjects; PARTIAL = characters/world covered but the prop has no visual reference yet (W-2 prop sheet pending); MISSING = no usable reference exists.

## Production notes

- **Hardest shots** (benchmark-relevant): SH06/SH07 (cloth), SH12/SH13 (hover gap, press + squash + *boing*), SH19/SH37 (dough-squeeze), SH22 (clonk timing), SH25 (wedged rain puddle), SH32 (inverted sitting), SH39 (lean contact). All are already flagged as benchmark hooks B2/B7/B8/B10 in the beat sheet.
- **Known tool-limit risks (class 3, from `RES-0012`):** sharp timing accents (the *boing*, the clonk snap, the dead-stop walks) and physical state transformations (squash-and-return) may soften in the current Kimi video tool; classify, don't redesign.
- **Props without visual references yet** (W-2 prop sheet pending): sheet, strap, pebbles, teapot/jug/straw, hand saw/drill, rope/pulley, butterfly, the Snail, Keep Shelf interior. The new chair is covered by the old-chair design visible in `R1.5_HILLTOP_3D_MASTER.jfif` (pegged ladder-back) — the twin is identical by story definition; any rendered twin is **DEVELOPMENT PROP — NOT FINAL CANON** until the W-2 sheet and owner approval.
- **Animatic assembly:** this plan is the required deliverable. A low-cost slideshow animatic can be assembled later from generated frames per sequence without changing this plan.
