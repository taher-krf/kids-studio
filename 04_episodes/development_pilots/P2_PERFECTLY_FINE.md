## Sound-off story

1. A baby bird falls out of its nest. Mulu's breezes help it practise flying, and they have the best time.
2. Tekla is left out. She won't say it. She tidies everything instead, more and more, even the Snail.
3. She builds the bird a ramp. The bird prefers Mulu. She curls up and rolls away.
4. Mulu follows the trail of tidied things to her and finally looks at *her*, not the sky. He stays with her, quietly.
5. The bird flies off her ramp *on* his breeze. At the end it sits on her head.

## Designed to be read without words

The hidden feeling is the whole test (`RSK-0005`), so the staging carries it:

- **Look → act.** Every Straightening is preceded by a held look at Mulu and the bird having fun. The cause is always on screen.
- **Foreground alone / background joy.** Tekla is staged foreground and still; the fun is staged background and moving. Four-year-olds read "left out" from this layout.
- **Escalation into absurdity.** Tidying tidy things is odd; tidying grass and a snail is *obviously* about something else. The oddness is the signal.
- **One anchor line for the youngest:** Mulu's "Not sunny." names it in his weather language. It is not a lesson and nobody explains it.

## Beats

### P2-01 · 0:00–0:20 · The Forecast · HT-03 → HT-04 → HT-02
- **Sees:** Mulu beams at the door. Tekla draws a big sun in his square. Hers stays empty. She sets out two teacups on the bench for morning tea.
- **Wants:** Tekla — their morning tea. Mulu — his day to start.
- **Gag:** Mulu leans into the chalk sun to admire himself.
- **Feeling info:** baseline: both content. The two cups are the plant.
- **Dialogue:** none.
- **Weather:** warm little breeze.
- **Tell:** none.
- **Complexity:** Low.
- **State:** morning · Forecast: sun · two cups full on the bench.

### P2-02 · 0:20–0:50 · The fall · HT-01 → HT-06
- **Sees:** High in the Lookout Tree, a fledgling flaps, wobbles, and tumbles out of its nest, fluttering down into the garden: *plop*. It isn't hurt; it's indignant. Mulu rushes over, hovering too close with worry. He tries something: a calm, careful breeze under its wings. The fledgling lifts a hand's width and lands. It looks at Mulu. It flaps at him: again!
- **Wants:** Mulu — to help. The fledgling — to fly. Tekla (arriving with a bucket and her folding ruler, prepared for anything) — to help too.
- **Gag:** Tekla arrives fully equipped for a problem that's already solved.
- **Feeling info:** Mulu's care makes his aim perfect (calm aims).
- **Dialogue:** none.
- **Weather:** calm, aimed breeze.
- **Tell:** none.
- **Complexity:** Medium — visitor asset (small bird: hop, flap, tumble, land). Nobody grabs or carries the bird.
- **State:** fledgling in the garden · nest in the Lookout Tree.

### P2-03 · 0:50–1:15 · The best game · HT-06 / plateau
- **Sees:** The fledgling rides Mulu's breezes in little arcs. Mulu spins with joy. "Up! Up! You did it!" Loops, hops, happy chaos. Tekla stands at the edge of frame with her bucket.
- **Wants:** Mulu and the fledgling — more! Tekla — (not yet visible) to be part of it.
- **Gag:** the bird demanding "again" with a flap every time.
- **Feeling info:** a brand-new best friendship, fast.
- **Dialogue:** "Up! Up! You did it!" — warmth.
- **Weather:** joy breezes (≤ 3 reacting objects: the fledgling, a leaf, the pinwheel).
- **Tell:** none yet.
- **Complexity:** Medium — bird flight cycle riding drawn wind.
- **State:** Mulu perk.

### P2-04 · 1:15–1:40 · Tea for one (the teaching beat) · HT-02
- **Sees:** Tekla at the bench with the two cups. She pours both. Background: Mulu and the bird spinning. She waits. She looks over at them, a held look. She drinks her tea. The second cup sits. She looks again. Then, without looking at it, her paw straightens the second cup a hair to the left. Then back.
- **Wants:** Tekla — Mulu to come for tea. Mulu — (unaware) to keep playing.
- **Gag:** the tiniest possible straightening.
- **Feeling info:** **the Straightening is taught here: look → feel → straighten.**
- **Dialogue:** none.
- **Weather:** background joy breezes only.
- **Tell:** the Straightening (1st, tiny).
- **Complexity:** Low — two planes of action; stage foreground still / background moving.
- **State:** cup 1 empty · cup 2 full, cooling.

### P2-05 · 1:40–1:55 · The invitation · HT-02 → garden edge
- **Sees:** Mulu zooms up: "Tekla! Look!" and waves her over. Tekla stands, walks past him, and says "Busy." She kneels and straightens the pebble border along the path, which is already straight. Mulu shrugs happily and zooms back.
- **Wants:** Mulu — to share his joy with her. Tekla — to join, but only if it doesn't look like she needs to.
- **Gag:** "Busy." with nothing to be busy with.
- **Feeling info:** she refuses what she wants (pride), so both contribute to the problem.
- **Dialogue:** "Tekla! Look!" / "Busy."
- **Weather:** a happy breeze as he zooms off.
- **Tell:** the Straightening (2nd).
- **Complexity:** Low.
- **State:** pebble border: straightened.

### P2-06 · 1:55–2:40 · The Straightening escalates (peak) · Garden / plateau montage
- **Sees:** Intercut. Foreground: Tekla straightens the garden stakes, lines pinecones up by size, combs the grass blade by blade with a comb, and finally, gently, turns the Snail so it faces the same way as the pebbles. Background: loops, spins and cheeping. Last shot: the Snail's eyestalks slowly swivel back to where they were.
- **Wants:** Tekla — to not feel it. Mulu — more flying.
- **Gag:** each object is less "tidyable" than the last: the grass, the Snail.
- **Feeling info:** children should be shouting "she's NOT fine!"
- **Dialogue:** none (music carries: Tekla's woodblock motif, quicker and quicker).
- **Weather:** background joy breezes.
- **Tell:** the Straightening (3rd–6th); tap-tap on the sound track.
- **Complexity:** Medium — many prop states. **Continuity manifest required** (benchmark B6).
- **State:** stakes aligned · pinecones in a size row · grass patch combed · Snail rotated to match the pebbles.

### P2-07 · 2:40–3:05 · The Sky Check · plateau
- **Sees:** Mulu floats over, glowing, to tell her everything. He checks the sky above her head: nothing. Clear. "Sunny!" He does a joy spin and zooms off. His spin gust scatters her pinecone row, tilts two stakes and flips a leaf. She stands in the mess. Then she re-straightens everything at twice the speed, a tidy blur.
- **Wants:** Mulu — to share. Tekla — for him to notice. He doesn't.
- **Gag:** the confident misread; the high-speed re-tidy.
- **Feeling info:** he's not unkind; he's reading the wrong place.
- **Dialogue:** "Sunny!"
- **Weather:** joy spin gust (**three-thing rule: pinecones, stakes, one leaf**) (benchmark B5).
- **Tell:** the Straightening (fast).
- **Complexity:** Medium.
- **State:** rows scattered → restored.

### P2-08 · 3:05–3:45 · The Build · plateau
- **Sees:** Tekla builds a flight ramp for the fledgling: a runway, a slope, a tiny flag and a picture-tag of a bird. It's far too much. She places the fledgling-sized "start" mark and steps back. The fledgling hops up the ramp, looks down it… hops off the side and flutters over to Mulu's breeze. The ramp stands empty, flag flapping.
- **Wants:** Tekla — a way in that doesn't need words. The fledgling — the fun one.
- **Gag:** the overbuilt ramp, and the bird ignoring it.
- **Feeling info:** she builds what she can't say.
- **Dialogue:** none.
- **Weather:** Mulu's breeze in the background (flag flaps).
- **Tell:** **the Build**.
- **Complexity:** Medium — a new prop (ramp) built on screen in cuts.
- **State:** ramp built (stays on the plateau for the rest of the episode).

### P2-09 · 3:45–4:00 · The Curl and the Roll · plateau → HT-02
- **Sees:** Tekla curls into a ball: *click*. One second. Then she rolls away across the flat plateau, neat and quick, to the bench. She uncurls, sits, very upright, and says to nobody: "Fine."
- **Wants:** Tekla — to disappear from the feeling.
- **Gag:** the dignified roll-away; "Fine." to an empty hill.
- **Feeling info:** overwhelmed. The older edge gets the irony; the youngest see a friend go away sad.
- **Dialogue:** "Fine."
- **Weather:** none near her.
- **Tell:** **the Curl** + **the Roll** (flat ground only; not a spin-dash).
- **Complexity:** Medium (benchmark B6).
- **State:** Tekla at the bench.

### P2-10 · 4:00–4:15 · She's gone · plateau
- **Sees:** The fledgling makes its best flight yet. Mulu turns, beaming, to share it, and Tekla isn't there. Just the empty ramp. He looks left, right, up. Nothing.
- **Wants:** Mulu — Tekla to see this.
- **Gag:** Mulu checking the sky for her (of course).
- **Feeling info:** the first time he misses her today.
- **Dialogue:** none.
- **Weather:** his breeze fades out.
- **Tell:** none.
- **Complexity:** Low.
- **State:** golden hour begins.

### P2-11 · 4:15–4:40 · The clue trail · garden → plateau → bench
- **Sees:** Mulu notices the Snail facing an odd direction, perfectly in line with the pebbles. Then the pinecones sorted by size. The combed grass. He squints, like a detective. He follows the tidy things one by one (the child has seen every one of them made). The trail ends at the bench.
- **Wants:** Mulu — to find her.
- **Gag:** detective Mulu.
- **Feeling info:** the child is ahead, and now Mulu catches up.
- **Dialogue:** none.
- **Weather:** none.
- **Tell:** the trail *is* her tells, seen from outside.
- **Complexity:** Low–Medium (the straightened props must match P2-06 exactly).
- **State:** Snail still rotated.

### P2-12 · 4:40–5:10 · Not sunny · HT-02
- **Sees:** Tekla on the bench, very upright, beside a cold, full teacup. Mulu does the Sky Check again: nothing. "…Sunny?" Then, for the first time, he looks at *her*, not the sky above her. Insert: her paw straightening her own apron pocket, over and over. Hold. "Not sunny." Tekla doesn't answer. A very small nod.
- **Wants:** Mulu — to understand. Tekla — to be understood without having to say it.
- **Gag:** none.
- **Feeling info:** the signature payoff: the audience's reading and Mulu's reading finally meet.
- **Dialogue:** "…Sunny?" / "Not sunny."
- **Weather:** none.
- **Tell:** the Straightening (her own pocket).
- **Complexity:** Low — a reaction-timing test.
- **State:** cup 2 full, cold.

### P2-13 · 5:10–5:20 · Sympathy drizzle · HT-02
- **Sees:** Feeling for her, Mulu drizzles. The drops land on her shell and roll off, beading down the bands. She watches a drop roll off. The corner of her mouth almost moves.
- **Wants:** Mulu — to feel it with her. Tekla — (quietly) to be felt with.
- **Gag:** none.
- **Feeling info:** the show's thesis image: his feelings can't hurt her.
- **Dialogue:** none.
- **Weather:** sad drizzle, rolling off her shell.
- **Tell:** none.
- **Complexity:** Medium — drops meeting a surface without wetting it (benchmark B3/B8).
- **State:** Tekla dry.

### P2-14 · 5:20–5:40 · The stillness (quiet moment) · HT-02 (+ reverse)
- **Sees:** He stops raining. He settles down beside her at talk height and stays: no bob, no breeze, no drip. He isn't vibrating with the effort; he's just still. Long hold (≥ 6 seconds). She leans, a tiny bit, toward him.
- **Wants:** both — this.
- **Gag:** none.
- **Feeling info:** the most important weather moment is no weather.
- **Dialogue:** none.
- **Weather:** **none** (deliberate absence).
- **Tell:** none.
- **Complexity:** Low to animate, high to time well.
- **State:** golden hour.

### P2-15 · 5:40–5:52 · Both ways · plateau
- **Sees:** The fledgling lands between them, then hops over to Tekla's ramp, runs down it and launches. At the end of the ramp, a small calm breeze from Mulu lifts it up and away. Tekla watches her ramp work. Then: evening tea. Mulu slurps the cold second cup in one go (a little plumper); she pours him another.
- **Wants:** the fledgling — to fly. Both — to have helped.
- **Gag:** the slurp.
- **Feeling info:** the bird simply chooses both. Nobody says anything about it.
- **Dialogue:** none.
- **Weather:** calm, aimed breeze.
- **Tell:** none.
- **Complexity:** Medium (bird on ramp + wind).
- **State:** both cups used · Mulu slightly plump.

### P2-16 · 5:52–6:00 · Button + Snail Check · HT-02 → garden
- **Sees:** The fledgling returns and perches on Tekla's head. Mulu does the Sky Check and finds a bird. "Sunny!" Tekla, with total dignity and a bird on her head: "…Fine." Snail Check: the Snail has turned itself back around. Tag: Tekla sets one pinecone on the Keep Shelf, crooked, on purpose.
- **Wants:** —
- **Gag:** the bird-hat; "Fine." now meaning fine.
- **Feeling info:** she's happy, and he read it correctly.
- **Dialogue:** "Sunny!" / "…Fine."
- **Weather:** a little happy breeze.
- **Tell:** none (the crooked pinecone is her growth).
- **Complexity:** Low–Medium (bird landing on a character).
- **State:** Snail faces its own way · Keep Shelf + crooked pinecone.

## Changes from the v0.2 concept (and why)

- **Readability repair for `RSK-0005`:** every Straightening is now preceded by an on-screen look at the cause, and the escalation ends in objects that can't be tidied (grass, the Snail).
- **Shared fault:** Mulu now invites her ("Tekla! Look!") and she refuses ("Busy."). Neither lead is unlikable; the jealousy is hers to hide and his to miss.
- **The Roll added** after the Curl (flat ground), which also motivates her absence in P2-10 and gives the benchmark a Roll shot.
- **One comprehension anchor** ("Not sunny.") replaces the purely wordless realization; Tekla still admits nothing out loud (a nod), which protects the season arc.
- **Button tightened:** "…Fine." now means fine. Same word, new meaning.

## Open questions

- Is the fledgling's fall (P2-02) gentle enough for the youngest viewers? Stage it as a flutter-down, never a drop.
- Does the Straightening read at 4 years without the "Not sunny." line? The animatic test should run once with the line and once without it.
