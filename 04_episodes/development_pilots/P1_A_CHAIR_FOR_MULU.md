## Sound-off story (what a child should be able to retell)

1. Tekla makes Mulu a chair and puts it next to her own chair by the bench.
2. Mulu can't sit: he floats. They try everything (measuring, a strap, pebbles, tea).
3. Tekla hangs the chair upside-down from the tree, and Mulu can "sit" in it up there. Success!
4. But now Tekla sits alone at sunset, next to an empty space. Mulu sees it from above and understands.
5. He comes down and becomes her backrest. The Snail ends up sitting in the hanging chair.

## Beats

### P1-01 · 0:00–0:20 · The Forecast · HT-03 → HT-04
- **Sees:** Morning. Tekla steps out; from the workshop behind her comes one last *tap-tap-tap* and a sheet-covered shape. Mulu bobs at the door, bouncing to see past her. She reads his weather (little skittery gusts) and draws a sun with swirl marks. Her own square stays empty. Title card.
- **Wants:** Mulu — to see what she's been building. Tekla — to keep it hidden until it's perfect.
- **Gag:** Every time Mulu leans to peek, Tekla steps precisely into his line of sight without looking.
- **Feeling info:** She's proud and secretive; he's excited.
- **Dialogue:** none.
- **Weather:** excited gusts (small; ruffle her chalk dust).
- **Tell:** none yet.
- **Complexity:** Low (ritual setups, reusable).
- **State:** morning light · Mulu normal / perk / smooth · chimes hung · Forecast: sun + swirls.

### P1-02 · 0:20–0:45 · The unveiling · HT-02
- **Sees:** Tekla leads Mulu by the nub to the bench. Beside her old chair stands a sheet-covered shape. Drumroll (she drums on her own shell). Sheet-pull! Mulu gasps with joy: a happy gust blows the sheet straight back over Tekla. She stands under it like a small, dignified ghost. Beat. She bows (the Tiny Bow, under the sheet). "Meant to do that."
- **Wants:** Tekla — a perfect reveal. Mulu — to see it.
- **Gag:** the ghost-Tekla bow.
- **Feeling info:** Mulu's joy is uncontrollable (weather he can't aim); Tekla's pride won't allow a mistake.
- **Dialogue:** "Meant to do that." (Tekla) — needed as the signature line.
- **Weather:** one happy gust (the sheet is the only reacting object).
- **Tell:** the Tiny Bow.
- **Complexity:** Medium — cloth. Fallback: a stiff canvas cover that flips over her like a lid. **Benchmark B10.**
- **State:** sheet: on chair → over Tekla → off · two chairs visible side by side (plant).

### P1-03 · 0:45–1:05 · "For me?!" · HT-02
- **Sees:** Tekla pulls the sheet off herself, folds it into a perfect square, and presents the chair with one paw: "Chair." It's a twin of her old chair. Mulu: "For me?!" Happy spin. Then he stops, looks at the chair, looks at his own flat bottom. He's never sat.
- **Wants:** Mulu — to love the gift properly. Tekla — for him to use it.
- **Gag:** she folds the sheet with precision while he spins.
- **Feeling info:** delight, then a flicker of worry (Top Puff tilts).
- **Dialogue:** "Chair." / "For me?!" — character and warmth.
- **Weather:** happy breeze during the spin (no objects react).
- **Tell:** none.
- **Complexity:** Low.
- **State:** Mulu perk → up.

### P1-04 · 1:05–1:30 · Sit #1: the gap · HT-02 (+ insert on the gap)
- **Sees:** Mulu lowers himself onto the seat very carefully and holds a proud "sitting" pose, hovering a finger's width above it, bobbing. "Look! Sitting!" Insert: the gap under him. Tekla sees the gap. She presses down on his top with both paws; he squashes onto the seat… and bounces back up: *boing*.
- **Wants:** Mulu — to make her happy by sitting. Tekla — the chair to work as designed.
- **Gag:** the proud pose over the visible gap (the child is ahead of Mulu).
- **Feeling info:** Mulu is trying hard; Tekla is quietly determined.
- **Dialogue:** "Look! Sitting!" — comedy (he isn't).
- **Weather:** none.
- **Tell:** Tekla's arms fold (she's thinking, not showing).
- **Complexity:** Medium — **hover-gap consistency is the joke and must read in every angle** (benchmark B2).
- **State:** Mulu hover gap ≈ 0.04 TH above the seat.

### P1-05 · 1:30–1:55 · The chair adapts · HT-02
- **Sees:** Tekla measures Mulu with her folding ruler: it sinks into his fluff and comes out wiggly. She studies the wiggle, nods, saws a little off one chair leg. Now the chair wobbles. Mulu tries again, hovering over a wobbling chair: "Sitting…?"
- **Wants:** Tekla — to fix it with engineering. Mulu — to keep trying for her.
- **Gag:** the wiggly ruler; a wobbly chair under a floating friend.
- **Feeling info:** Tekla treats the problem as a building problem, because that's what she knows.
- **Dialogue:** "Sitting…?" — rule of three (doubt).
- **Weather:** none.
- **Tell:** none.
- **Complexity:** Low–Medium — ruler sinking into Mulu is a designed contact (prop into soft body, returns to model).
- **State:** chair leg: sawn (the offcut is set aside → Keep Shelf later) · chair wobble ON.

### P1-06 · 1:55–2:20 · The strap · HT-02
- **Sees:** Tekla buckles an apron strap around Mulu and the chair back. Mulu squeezes out through the gap like dough and pops back into shape above the seat. The strap hangs empty and neatly buckled.
- **Wants:** Tekla — to hold him down. Mulu — to cooperate (he really is trying).
- **Gag:** dough-squeeze and pop.
- **Feeling info:** nobody is upset yet; it's a puzzle.
- **Dialogue:** none.
- **Weather:** none.
- **Tell:** none.
- **Complexity:** Medium — **squash through a gap and exact return to model** (benchmark B2).
- **State:** Mulu squash → normal · strap buckled, empty.

### P1-07 · 2:20–2:55 · Pebbles and the butterfly (signature beat) · HT-02
- **Sees:** Tekla fills Mulu's nubs with pebbles. He sinks, slowly, triumphantly, toward the seat. Concentration face. A butterfly enters frame-left and flutters toward him (the child sees it long before he does). Almost… Mulu spots the butterfly, gasps with delight: a happy gust lifts him; the pebbles drop onto Tekla's shell: *clonk* (×3, rhythmic). Hold. "I'm fine." She turns and straightens the chair, which is already perfectly straight.
- **Wants:** Mulu — to stay heavy. Tekla — to finish the plan.
- **Gag:** anticipation (butterfly) → clonk-clonk-clonk → deadpan.
- **Feeling info:** she's frustrated and hides it; the Straightening shows it.
- **Dialogue:** "I'm fine." — the cover-irony line.
- **Weather:** happy gust (reacting objects: the butterfly, a leaf).
- **Tell:** **the Straightening** (first of the episode).
- **Complexity:** Medium — **comic timing to the frame: clonk → hold (≥ 18 frames at 24 fps) → line → straighten** (benchmark B7).
- **State:** pebbles: in nubs → on ground around Tekla · Mulu normal · Tekla dry, unhurt ("pain stops at clonk").

### P1-08 · 2:55–3:35 · Tea (Mulu's idea; physical-comedy peak) · HT-02
- **Sees:** Mulu has the idea: he grabs the long straw and drinks the whole teapot. Plumper. Tekla, catching on, brings the water jug. He drinks that too. Now he's plump and heavy, and he settles into the chair, properly, wedged. Victory! Both freeze in triumph. He's so happy that the extra water comes out as happy rain, straight into the seat. He's sitting in a puddle of his own rain, beaming. Tekla produces a hand drill and drills a drain hole. "Plan." The water drains out (Tekla sidesteps the trickle without looking). Rained-out and back to normal size, Mulu floats gently up out of the chair. "Not sitting."
- **Wants:** Mulu — to solve it himself. Tekla — to not lose the victory.
- **Gag:** the proud face while raining into his own seat; the drain hole; the slow float-up.
- **Feeling info:** real joy for both, briefly. Then back to square one, with no blame.
- **Dialogue:** "Plan." / "Not sitting." — character and the rule-of-three payoff.
- **Weather:** happy rain (under his base only; into the seat).
- **Tell:** none (she's busy solving).
- **Complexity:** Medium–High — capacity states (normal → plump → normal), straw contact, puddle in the seat, drain trickle. Drinking is a gag here, not physics.
- **State:** Mulu capacity plump → normal · teapot and jug empty · seat puddle → drained · chair: drain hole · Tekla dry.

### P1-09 · 3:35–3:50 · The flip · HT-02 → HT-01
- **Sees:** Tekla stares at the chair. Then up at Mulu, floating. Then the chair. She tips her head slowly to one side, then all the way over. She looks up at the Lookout branch. "Hmm."
- **Wants:** Tekla — the idea that makes the chair work.
- **Gag:** her head-tilt continuing further than seems possible.
- **Feeling info:** she refuses to give up; it's pride *and* love.
- **Dialogue:** "Hmm." — marks the idea.
- **Weather:** none.
- **Tell:** none.
- **Complexity:** Low.
- **State:** golden hour begins (sun lower; standard light change).

- **Wants:** Tekla — to make it work by building. Mulu — to watch her do it (he's impressed).
- **Gag:** the Snail riding up unnoticed (the child sees it).
- **Feeling info:** Tekla's competence; Mulu's admiration.

- **State:** Mulu at Lookout height.

## Changes from the v0.2 concept (and why)

- **Plant made visible.** The new chair is placed *next to her old chair* in the unveiling, so the real want is on screen from 0:45, and the sunset payoff is fair.
- **Escalation reordered by kind** (who adapts to whom) instead of seven similar attempts. The "measuring" and "saw" beats merged.
- **The upside-down chair now creates the problem:** success at sitting separates them. That irony drives the emotional turn. Previously the success and the realization were disconnected.
- **Safety:** the chair goes up by rope and pulley (no climbing); Mulu drinks tea and a water jug, not a watering can.
- **The Snail button** moved into the hanging chair (the twin chair is no longer at the bench).
- **Words:** a "sitting" rule of three added because it improves comedy and comprehension. 20 words total, below the 30–100 working range on purpose: the sound-off story is complete, and no line was added just to reach a number.
