## Sound-off story

1. Mulu secretly makes Tekla a present (wind chimes) and wraps it in a leaf. The moment it's a secret, he starts to drip.
2. The drips leave a trail to wherever he hides it. Tekla sees and pretends not to, stepping over every drop.
3. Mulu tries to stop the trail: holding it in (side-drips), then catching the drips in his umbrella, which gets heavier and heavier.
4. Tekla, "not looking", walks into the umbrella. Splash. She stays dry. Meanwhile the Snail follows the wet trail and nibbles the leaf wrapping.
5. At sunset Mulu, sure it's ruined, gives it to her. She fakes a terrible gasp, then shows the hook she made for it this morning. She knew. The chimes ring with his happiness.

## Beats

### P3-01 · 0:00–0:20 · The secret is made (pre-title) · Hilltop, umbrella bed, dawn
- **Sees:** First light. Mulu, alone in his umbrella bed, finishes something: three spoons, two shells and a pebble tied to a stick with string. He wraps it in a big leaf and ties it. He looks at it, glowing. And the instant it becomes a secret, one drop falls from one spot on his base: *plink*. He looks down at the drop. "Shh!" Another drop.
- **Wants:** Mulu — to give Tekla a surprise at sunset.
- **Gag:** shushing his own weather.
- **Feeling info:** **the code is taught: secret → the drip.** The child is ahead of Tekla from here on.
- **Dialogue:** "Shh!"
- **Weather:** the Secret Drip starts (one drop, steady rhythm).
- **Tell:** —
- **Complexity:** Medium — drip start on cue (benchmark B3); chimes prop in its wrapped state.
- **State:** dawn · present: wrapped · drip ON (rate: slow).

### P3-02 · 0:20–0:40 · The Forecast · HT-03 → HT-04
- **Sees:** Tekla steps out with her chalk. Mulu is hovering by the door with a very wide smile, dripping. *plink… plink.* She looks at the drip, then at him. "Nothing!" he says, to no question. "Hmm." She draws a single drop and a question mark. Title card.
- **Wants:** Mulu — to act normal. Tekla — to know what's going on.
- **Gag:** "Nothing!" unprompted.
- **Feeling info:** she has noticed; he thinks she hasn't.
- **Dialogue:** "Nothing!" / "Hmm."
- **Weather:** Secret Drip.
- **Tell:** none (she's reading him).
- **Complexity:** Low.
- **State:** morning · Forecast: drop + "?".

### P3-03 · 0:40–1:10 · The hiding · HT-01 → Workshop (WS-01)
- **Sees:** When Tekla turns to the garden, Mulu sneaks the leaf-present into her workshop and tucks it behind the bits bin. Wide: a neat line of drips from the umbrella bed, across the lawn, straight to the workshop door.
- **Wants:** Mulu — a perfect hiding place.
- **Gag:** the hiding place is the one room she uses most.
- **Feeling info:** the child knows exactly where the present is.
- **Dialogue:** none.
- **Weather:** drip; the trail forms.
- **Tell:** —
- **Complexity:** Medium — **wet trail persists across consecutive shots** (benchmark B4).
- **State:** present: workshop · trail A: bed → workshop door.

### P3-04 · 1:10–1:35 · She sees, and decides · HT-01
- **Sees:** Tekla turns back and sees the trail. Hold. She follows it with her eyes to the workshop door. She looks at Mulu, who is humming at the sky. Beat. Then, deliberately, she looks away at the horizon. She walks across the lawn stepping over every single drop like a laser beam, humming flatly. At the start of the trail, the Snail appears, attracted by the wet.
- **Wants:** Tekla — to protect his surprise. (It's the only way a closed book can say "I love that you made me something.")
- **Gag:** laser-beam stepping; two friends humming at different skies.
- **Feeling info:** **now both are keeping a secret, for each other.** The child is in on both.
- **Dialogue:** none (humming).
- **Weather:** drip.
- **Tell:** —
- **Complexity:** Low–Medium (trail continuity).
- **State:** Snail at the start of trail A.

### P3-05 · 1:35–2:05 · Moving it · Workshop → Garden
- **Sees:** Tekla heads for her workshop to fetch a tool. Panic! Mulu zips in ahead of her, grabs the present and zips out the other way, leaving a new zigzag trail. As he goes, a spoon handle pokes out of the leaf; Tekla glimpses it (so does the child). He stuffs the present under the big cabbage leaves in the garden. Tekla glances his way. "Nothing!"
- **Wants:** Mulu — a better hiding place. Tekla — her tool (and now she knows what the present is).
- **Gag:** the zigzag trail; "Nothing!" (2).
- **Feeling info:** the spoon glimpse is the plant for the hook.
- **Dialogue:** "Nothing!"
- **Weather:** drip, faster (worry).
- **Tell:** —
- **Complexity:** Medium (two trails now; trail A fades by the end of the scene).
- **State:** present: under the cabbage leaves · trail B: workshop → garden (zigzag) · drip rate: medium.

### P3-06 · 2:05–2:30 · Holding it in · Garden / plateau
- **Sees:** Mulu decides to stop the drip by holding it in. He takes a deep breath and holds it: cheeks puffed, eyes squeezed, body inflating. The drip stops underneath… and pops out of his *side* instead, sideways, *plink* onto a leaf. He inflates more. A tiny squeak-puff escapes.
- **Wants:** Mulu — no more trail.
- **Gag:** suppression backfire: the drip comes out somewhere stranger.
- **Feeling info:** hiding a feeling makes it show more.
- **Dialogue:** none.
- **Weather:** side-drip; a giggle-sized puff.
- **Tell:** —
- **Complexity:** Medium — the held-breath state and a side-drip from a fixed point (benchmark B3).
- **State:** Mulu held (inflated) → normal · drip source: side → base.

### P3-07 · 2:30–3:05 · The catching umbrella · HT-01 / HT-03
- **Sees:** New plan. Mulu lifts his umbrella bed off its post and carries it bowl-up beneath himself, pinched by the rim in both nubs, catching every drop. No trail! He's delighted and flies a proud lap. But the umbrella fills, *plink* by *plink*, and gets heavier, so through the scene he sinks lower and lower, until he's bumping along the grass at Tekla height.
- **Wants:** Mulu — to be clever.
- **Gag:** a cloud carrying an umbrella for himself, the wrong way up; the slow sink.
- **Feeling info:** pride, then effort.
- **Dialogue:** none.
- **Weather:** drip into the umbrella.
- **Tell:** —
- **Complexity:** High — a prop carried beneath a floating character, with a rising water level (benchmark B8).
- **State:** umbrella bed: off the post · water level: empty → ¼ → ½ → ¾ · Mulu's float height sinking.

### P3-08 · 3:05–3:30 · The "I am not looking" screen · Workshop (WS-01)
- **Sees:** Tekla sets up a folding screen around her workbench with a closed-eye picture-tag on it. Behind it (we see; Mulu doesn't) she's making something small from wire and a peg. We don't learn what it is.
- **Wants:** Tekla — to keep not-noticing, and to get something ready.
- **Gag:** a sign that says, in pictures, "not looking".
- **Feeling info:** a plant: she's up to something too.
- **Dialogue:** none.
- **Weather:** —
- **Tell:** tap-tap while she works (sound).
- **Complexity:** Low–Medium (new prop: the screen).
- **State:** screen: up · hook: made (hidden in her apron pocket).

### P3-09 · 3:30–4:00 · Bonk · plateau
- **Sees:** Tekla crosses the lawn with her apron pulled up over her eyes so she can't possibly see anything. She walks straight into Mulu's low-hanging umbrella: *bonk*. It tips. A big splash drenches the grass, the Forecast Board and Mulu's own face, but not her: the water beads and rolls off her shell. Beat. "I'm fine." She straightens her apron. Mulu, dripping into a puddle, to nobody: "Nothing!"
- **Wants:** Tekla — to prove she's not looking. Mulu — to recover.
- **Gag:** the bonk; everything wet except her; "Nothing!" (3) to a puddle.
- **Feeling info:** her kindness is causing its own chaos.
- **Dialogue:** "I'm fine." / "Nothing!"
- **Weather:** splash (not Mulu's rain; the umbrella's water), then his drip.
- **Tell:** the Straightening.
- **Complexity:** High — a splash, then water rolling off a character without wetting her (showcase shot; benchmark B8).
- **State:** umbrella: empty, tipped · puddle on the lawn (dries by the next scene) · Mulu back to float height · Tekla dry.

- **Sees:** Tekla produces the biggest, worst fake-surprised face in the history of the hill: both paws up, eyes enormous. "Gasp!" Mulu stops drizzling in shock. Beat. He squints at her. That was a terrible gasp.

That makes him happier, so he breezes more, so they ring more… The feedback loop ends with the chimes spinning, Tekla's apron flapping and her ears blown flat. Deadpan: "…Fine."

## Changes from the v0.2 concept (and why)

- **Pre-title "secret is made" beat.** The child learns the drip code and knows the secret before Tekla does, which makes every later beat forecast irony.
- **Tekla's hook** (built behind her "not looking" screen, planted by the spoon glimpse) makes the double reveal visual and fair. Previously her knowing was only implied.
- **The confession** now follows the Snail's nibbling (the child anticipates it for three minutes), and her fake "Gasp!" rescues the surprise. Previously the reveal leaned on explanation.
- **"Nothing!"** runs as a rule of three and breaks on the third (said to a puddle).
- **The umbrella splash** is the showcase proof of "rain rolls off her shell".

## Open questions

- The splash (P3-09) is the most expensive shot across the three pilots. Fallback: the umbrella tips *away* from camera and we see only the aftermath and a dry Tekla.
- If P3 does not air first, the chimes already hang by the door and the gift becomes something else; the drip engine is unchanged.
